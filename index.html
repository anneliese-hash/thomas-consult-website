Navigating GTM bottlenecks in B2B SaaS and fintech

Most scale-up founders and CEOs face three critical revenue bottlenecks:

01

Escalating CAC & pipeline stagnation

Traditional outbound and inbound playbooks lose yield while enterprise buying committees become more risk-averse, lengthening sales cycles.

02

AI & search invisibility (GEO/AEO)

Standard SEO no longer guarantees discovery as buyers turn to generative engines and LLMs (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) to research solutions and vendor shortlists.

03

Revenue leakage & operational noise

Misaligned GTM teams run fragmented, inefficient campaigns rather than protecting Net Revenue Retention (NRR) and expanding core accounts.

Measuring success through board-level financial metrics

Marketing should never be evaluated on vanity metrics. I measure commercial performance through strict financial discipline:

  • Capital efficiency: Shorter CAC payback periods and higher marketing yield per spend.
  • Pipeline velocity: Accelerated deal progression across target ICP accounts.
  • Net revenue retention (NRR): Plugging revenue leakage and systematically scaling account expansion.
  • AI share of voice: Establishing category citation authority across emerging generative AI search platforms.
